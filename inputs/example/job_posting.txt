Machine Learning Engineer
Amsterdam, Netherlands · Meridian Analytics · Permanent

About the role

Meridian Analytics builds forecasting and pricing tools for mid-sized logistics companies across
Europe. We're hiring a Machine Learning Engineer to join our small, senior Data team, working
directly with our Head of Data Science and two other engineers.

You'll own the full lifecycle of our demand-forecasting models: from feature engineering against
messy, real-world shipment data, through training and evaluation, to deployment and monitoring in
production. You'll also help us build out our first LLM-powered features — an internal assistant
that lets ops teams query forecasts and pricing recommendations in plain language.

What you'll do

- Design, train and maintain forecasting models used directly in daily pricing decisions.
- Build evaluation pipelines that catch model regressions before they reach production, not after.
- Prototype and ship LLM-based tooling for internal users, with proper grounding and guardrails
  rather than an ungrounded chatbot bolted onto a dashboard.
- Work closely with the ops team to understand where model outputs are trusted, ignored, or
  actively worked around — and fix the underlying reason, not just the symptom.
- Contribute to our engineering practices: code review, testing, and keeping production systems
  observable and debuggable.

What we're looking for

- Production experience shipping and maintaining ML systems, not just training notebooks.
- Comfort working with imperfect, real-world data and building pipelines that degrade gracefully
  rather than silently producing garbage.
- Some hands-on experience with LLM-based tooling: prompting, structured output, evaluation, or
  agentic pipelines.
- Strong communication — you'll regularly need to explain model behavior to non-technical
  stakeholders who make real business decisions based on it.

Why Meridian

We're a 12-person team serving a niche most "AI-first" logistics startups ignore: companies too
small for enterprise software vendors to bother with, but too complex for spreadsheets. Every
model we ship is used by someone whose job gets measurably easier or harder depending on whether
it's any good — there's no vanity metric standing between our work and its consequences.
